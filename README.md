# TaskyXPz 🚀

### Tasks + Experience. Finish your tasks, earn XP, level up your week.

TaskyXPz is officially **live**. It's a task manager that turns your to-do list into a game: every task you complete gives you XP, and a progress bar shows how close you are to beating your week.

**See the launch page:** https://mattxcreativework.github.io/taskyxpz-launch/

## Why TaskyXPz?

Most to-do apps just list what you haven't done. TaskyXPz celebrates what you have.

- ⚡ Earn XP for every finished task
- 📈 Track your weekly goal with a progress bar
- 🔒 Your tasks stay saved in your own browser, no account needed

## About this page

This launch page is a CSS-focused project: a bold, original design built only with HTML and CSS, in a dark blue and purple neon style.

- Neon sign title with glow and flicker
- Scrolling ticker banner built with keyframes
